# thread0 0.1.212

This release makes the phone-to-PC connection self-healing, driven by a real incident: a desktop whose sync stream silently died kept running for hours while every phone reported "the PC seems to be off," and nothing short of restarting the app would fix it. It also completes the thread0 rebrand across the desktop app and ships the interface in nine languages.

The desktop now watches its own presence heartbeat. A write that hangs is surfaced within seconds, and after a sustained drought the app rebuilds its sync client and re-subscribes every listener — pairing requests, guest grants, and policy watchers all come back without a restart. The web app does its part too: it no longer equates a missing presence signal with a powered-off PC, retries with its own recovery when signaling fails repeatedly, and always offers reconnect, refresh, and PC switching instead of a dead end. A renderer crash on the desktop, which used to leave a black window, now recovers by reloading the display layer automatically.

Sheets on the phone (session menu, start options, folder picker) are now immune to an iOS quirk where a sheet opened inside a scrolled list would drift off-screen with no way to scroll back — every sheet now mounts at the document root, so its header and close button are always reachable. Long sheets keep their title pinned and scroll internally.

The interface now ships in Korean, English, Spanish, Portuguese, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Japanese, German, and French. Roughly 450 new strings were moved into the shared catalog this release — guest screens, session management, file browsing, usage and limits, approval cards, and connection errors included — and a regression gate now blocks any new hardcoded copy from creeping in. Test runs pin their locale, so results no longer depend on the machine's language.

## Highlights

- **Self-healing connections** — the desktop detects a wedged sync stream (15s write deadline, 150s watchdog), rebuilds its client, and re-subscribes all listeners; the phone stops mis-reporting a live PC as "off" and recovers on its own with a 2-minute cooldown.
- **No more black windows** — a crashed display layer reloads automatically (bounded retries), and GPU/process failures are now recorded for diagnosis.
- **Phone sheets always reachable** — sheets render at the document root, sidestepping the iOS fixed-position-in-scroller bug; headers and close buttons can no longer scroll out of reach.
- **Nine-language interface** — full catalog coverage enforced at compile time; ~450 strings localized this release; a ratchet test prevents regressions.
- **thread0 rebrand completed** — remaining AgentDeck branding in the home screen removed; remote.thread0.ai is live end to end.
