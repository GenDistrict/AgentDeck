# AgentDeck 0.1.183

AgentDeck 0.1.183 is a stable release that makes the device hierarchy tangible on the phone: you can always see which computer you are controlling, tell your computers apart by their real names, and move between them with one tap.

## Highlights

### Your computers, by name

- Computers now advertise their real machine name (hostname) to your phone instead of a generic label, so a list of several PCs is finally tellable apart. Each computer starts advertising its name once it runs this version.
- The device-pairing panel on the PC shows the same name, so you know exactly what to look for on the phone.

### The phone title is the computer switcher

- The title at the top of the phone app now shows which computer you are controlling. Tap it to see every online computer on your AgentDeck account and switch with one tap.
- Switching to a computer that already approved this phone connects immediately — the six-digit safety code no longer flashes by. The code only appears when a computer truly needs a first-time approval.
- Connecting a second computer is now phone-led: no QR needed. The QR on the PC is only for opening the web app the very first time.

### Operations console

- Per-user journey rows expand into an account → devices tree (PCs first, then phones).
- Orphaned devices (whose owner account was deleted) are detected and called out instead of silently inflating device counts.
