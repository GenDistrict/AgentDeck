# AgentDeck 0.1.182

AgentDeck 0.1.182 is a stable release that rebuilds the first-run journey around consent and continuity: the app finds what you already have, asks before touching anything, and gets you to your first reply with taps instead of typing.

## Highlights

### Onboarding that starts from what you already have

- On first run, AgentDeck offers to check whether Claude or GPT is already signed in on this computer — read-only, and only after you agree. If a sign-in is found, one tap connects a copy; the original files are never touched, and the real CLI verifies the result before anything is called connected.
- After connecting, AgentDeck offers to look up your recent terminal work. One tap resumes a past conversation with its full history, or starts a session that explores one of your own project folders — no blank-page moment.
- Declining any of these offers is remembered permanently. Nothing re-asks.
- If you leave the setup card idle, a single desktop reminder is sent once — never repeated.

### Fewer interruptions by default

- New sessions now default to automatic tool approval by a risk classifier. Irreversible actions still always ask a person, and any mode you choose yourself is respected everywhere.

### A visible device hierarchy

- The phone app now always shows which PC it is controlling, and tapping it switches to any other PC on your account — previously possible in principle, now visible and one tap away.
- The device-pairing panel shows the hierarchy plainly: your AgentDeck account is just the key that lets your PCs and phone find each other; chats, files, and AI accounts stay on each PC. Terminology is now consistent everywhere: "AgentDeck account" vs "AI accounts".

### Fixes

- Fixed a bug where importing an existing CLI sign-in succeeded but the screen never advanced past "Connecting the account…" — found by the packaged end-to-end release verification for this build.
- The waiting banner no longer narrates internal details when no response card is present; it simply says the agent is waiting for you.
