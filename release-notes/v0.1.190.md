# AgentDeck 0.1.190

AgentDeck 0.1.190 adds guest invitations — hand a session to an outside developer with a single link, no account required — and opens the full set of native Claude and GPT CLI capabilities inside AgentDeck.

## Highlights

### Guest invitations (outside developers)

- **One link, no account.** Create an invite from Settings → Connected devices with a role — Observe (read-only), Collaborate, or Full — and a duration. The guest opens the link, types a name, and waits for your approval. Invites are single-use, expire in 72 hours if unredeemed, and are capped at 10 active per owner.
- **Approval is the gate.** Both screens show the same 6-digit code; nothing connects — not even a relay slot — until you approve on the PC. Declining voids the invite permanently.
- **Roles are enforced server- and PC-side.** Observe can only read; Collaborate cannot hand off accounts or switch engines; even Full cannot unregister your workspaces. Access ends the second it expires or you revoke it.
- **Activity view per guest.** A single screen shows a timeline of everything the guest did — session turns, file transfers, denied attempts, permission requests — plus a direct chat between you and the guest. Permission requests (file transfer, folder access) can be granted inline from the timeline.
- **Guests see their limits.** A persistent guest bar shows the PC name, role and time remaining; when transfer is off the attach button says why and offers a one-tap permission request. On expiry the guest gets a clear end screen and the device cache is wiped.
- **Everything is audited**, including downloads and uploads over the encrypted relay path.

### Native CLI capabilities, fully opened

- **Slash-command palette and @-file mentions** in the composer, for both engines.
- **File rewind** — restore files to the state at any of your messages, with a preview of what will change before you confirm.
- **Session forking** — branch a conversation to try a different approach; the original transcript is untouched.
- **Reasoning effort and thinking budget** can be changed mid-session and apply from the next turn.
- **MCP OAuth and form prompts** flow through approval cards; connector status (connected/failed) with one-tap reconnect.
- **Plugin management** — install, enable/disable, remove, and add/remove marketplaces.
- **Subagent visibility** — watch a Task's sub-agent conversation live inside the tool block.
- **Additional folders (--add-dir)** — explicitly grant a session access to folders outside its workspace.
- **GPT review targets** — review uncommitted changes, against a branch, or a specific commit; **GPT skills** can be attached to a session; **routines now run on both Claude and GPT accounts**.

### Fixes worth knowing

- Messages you sent from the phone no longer appear twice (or out of order) after reconnecting or updating — transcript identity is now unified on engine UUIDs.
- The web app recovers in place from the rare "Could not open the screen" crash — the reload button now clears the broken cache instead of looping, and chunk-ordering faults were removed at the source.
- Long file names and paths no longer push the desktop side panel off-screen; the plan-limit chip stays on one line; the guest activity view renders correctly while signed out.
- Artifacts and files open with the OS default application, exactly like Explorer/Finder — executable types ask for confirmation first.
- macOS notification diagnostics: a test-notification button in Settings and startup-log traces to pinpoint permission issues.
